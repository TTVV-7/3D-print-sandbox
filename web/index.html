Realtor NFC cards

Three fields, two sides, one tap.

A long line shrinks to fit. Past a point that stops being printable — put a | where it should break instead, and both halves stay full size.

Raised border (card only)

How far the lettering stands off the face. 0.6 mm reads; 1.2 mm you can feel with a thumb; past 2 mm the thin strokes start to snag.

Tap side

Use | to split lines. Leave it empty for arcs alone — which is what the fob gets anyway, there is no room for text beside its pocket.

Millimetres, measured off your own tags. The pocket adds 0.4 mm of slack, and the fob grows if it has to.

Split gives you two parts on one plate, each with half the cavity and a three-pin key so they only go together one way. The tag ends up on the neutral plane with plastic either side.

Nothing here writes the tag — that is a phone job (NFC Tools and the like). This just counts the bytes it would take.

Colours

Written into the 3MF as two materials, so the slicer opens it with the lettering already on the second filament. The STL is one solid; its split is where the colour change goes.
