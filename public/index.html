Who

Goes on the front beside the name. Flat fills only — strokes, gradients and text objects are ignored, so export the logo as outlines first. Each fill colour in the file goes to whichever of the four colours below it is nearest.

Replaces name, company, phone, logo and pattern with your own artwork, filling the face. Draw it on a rectangle in the body colour and it comes out at the rectangle's size.

Look

Colours

Every colour is an inlay in one thin face layer — the first 0.6 mm of each half as it prints — so a multi-material printer does all its changes in three layers and the rest of the part in the body colour. Primary is the name, phone and logo; secondary the company line, rule and tap wording. The 3MF carries all four; the STL is one solid.

Relief
flush

How far the lettering stands off the face. Flush is a smooth card with every colour in the face layer. 1.2 mm you can feel with a thumb, at the cost of a colour change on every layer of the relief.

Body

Two halves print face down on one plate, each with half the cavity and a three-pin key so they only go together one way. Glue the flat border outside the cavity and clamp; the tag ends up sealed in the middle with nothing showing.

Millimetres, measured off your own tags. The cavity adds 0.4 mm of slack, and the fob grows if it has to.

Tap side

Use | to split lines. Leave it empty for arcs alone — which is what the fob gets anyway, there is no room for text beside its cavity.

Nothing here writes the tag — that is a phone job (NFC Tools and the like). This counts the bytes it would take.

For phones that don't tap. A shorter link is a coarser code, and a coarser code prints on a bigger nozzle — the readout says which. Scanners like dark modules on a light ground; a light code on a dark body is inverted, which most phones read and some don't.

Batch

Name, company, phone, link — commas or tabs, so a spreadsheet pastes straight in. Company and link may be blank. While there is anything here, the preview and the downloads are the whole batch on one plate, sharing the settings above.
