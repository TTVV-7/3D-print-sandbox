## Title

**Excavator Bucket Key Holder: Wall Mount, 2-Colour, Strong Teeth**

(For the branded version: *SWC Excavator Bucket Key Holder*)

## Summary (one line)

A wall-mounted excavator bucket with teeth to hang your keys on and a bowl for
everything else, marked up like an engineering drawing.

## Description

Hang your keys on the teeth and drop your wallet, sunglasses or phone in the
bucket. It mounts with two screws through keyholes on the back.

**Built not to snap.** The teeth aren't glued-on colour blocks. They grow out
of a full-width black cutting-edge plate that's lap-jointed into the bucket
floor, so there's no weak colour seam where a tooth meets the bucket. Each
tooth also has a 2 mm channel down its middle. Push in a 2 mm nail, or a
piece of 1.75 mm filament with a drop of super glue, for a steel- or
filament-reinforced tooth.

**A drawing of itself.** Every flat face is marked up like a shop drawing,
and every number is a real dimension of the part:
- The side elevation is dimensioned on each side plate: 80 high, 58 deep, R26 heel, 58° mouth.
- A ruler runs across the top, with 5 mm ticks up the front edges.
- The back wall shows the 140 inside width and the 104 keyhole spacing.
- The tooth pitch and notes are in yellow on the black edge plate.
- A title block sits underneath.

All the artwork is inlaid flush, not raised, so it prints as a clean colour
change and stays smooth.

**Size:** 150 x 91 x 94 mm. The keyholes are 104 mm apart and take #8 / 4 mm
screws with heads up to 9 mm.

## Print settings

Print it on its back, exactly as it loads. It needs no supports. Load
filament 1 = yellow and filament 2 = black.

| | **Standard** | **Strong** (recommended for PLA) |
|---|---|---|
| File | `*_standard.3mf` | `*_strong.3mf` |
| Walls | 4 | 6 |
| Infill | 20% gyroid | 40% gyroid |
| Teeth + edge plate (black part) | 20% | **100% (solid)** |
| Top / bottom layers | 5 / 4 | 6 / 5 |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm | 0.2 mm |

The walls and infill are already set in each 3MF as per-object settings.
Check them in your slicer's object list before you slice.

**PLA tips:** print the nozzle 5–10 °C hotter than your usual, and turn part
cooling down a little (to about 60–70%). Both help the layers in the teeth
bond. PETG is tougher still if you have it.

**No multi-material printer?** Print `stl/*_single-colour.stl` in one colour.
The artwork is flush with the surface, so it simply disappears.

## Files

- `*_standard.3mf`, `*_strong.3mf`: two-colour projects with the print settings set
- `stl/*_yellow.stl`, `stl/*_black.stl`: the two colours, for slicers that want separate STLs (they line up when loaded together)
- `stl/*_single-colour.stl`: the whole thing as one part
- `images/`: a 4:3 cover and front, side, top and underside views
